\# Bokhyllan Pilot Library v1



Purpose:



This is not a launch library.



This is not a "best books" list.



This is a validation library.



The goal is to test whether Bokhyllan's enrichment model can create useful, consistent, and scalable enrichment drafts across multiple literary categories, emotional registers, and reading situations.

Validation Goals



\* Multiple genres

\* Multiple emotional registers

\* Swedish and international authors

\* Literary and accessible books

\* Fiction and non-fiction

\* Books Bokhyllan would likely recommend

\* Books Bokhyllan might reject

\* Books at the edge of the concept



Success is not agreement.



Success is discovering whether the enrichment model produces believable results across all 25 books.
